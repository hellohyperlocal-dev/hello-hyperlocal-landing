# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: Suburb residents who want to stay informed about trusted local news, safety notices, community events, lost pets, and resident-only deals from nearby independent businesses.

Secondary audience: Local independent merchant owners (cafes, bakeries, shops, neighbourhood markets) seeking a direct connection to verified nearby residents without corporate ad algorithms.

## Product Purpose

Hello Hyperlocal is a trusted neighbourhood platform designed to help people love where they live. It connects verified residents, independent businesses, and community organizations into an authentic digital town square that strengthens local pride, safety, and mutual support.

## Positioning

A verified, spam-free neighbourhood platform that connects real residents and independent local businesses right outside their front door.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Download the app on Apple App Store or Google Play.
- Secondary CTA: Local business partnership exploration and suburb expansion interest.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Love where you live. Your neighbourhood in your pocket."
- Belief ladder:
  1. My suburb has vibrant people, independent shops, and community events that I want to discover and support.
  2. Mainstream social networks and local message boards are noisy, toxic, unverified, and filled with ads.
  3. Hello Hyperlocal is 100% verified, privacy-first, spam-free, and always free for residents.
  4. Getting the app gives me an instant, trusted connection to what is happening on my street.
- Proof on hand: Verified partner logos from local hubs (`public/partner-logos/`: Goddess Cafe, Linden Community Association, Linden Lanes, Linden Market) and authentic neighbourhood photography (`public/photography/linden-market.jpg`).

## Brand Personality

Warm, optimistic, neighbourly, authentic, modern, welcoming, community-first. It feels like the friendly greeting of your favourite local coffee shop.

## Anti-references

- Toxic social media groups and unverified local forums full of anonymous complaints.
- Cluttered municipal notices and classified advertisement boards.
- Cold, corporate SaaS landing pages filled with generic enterprise jargon.

## Design Principles

1. Human-Centered Warmth: Design with warmth, approachable typography, and friendly micro-interactions that make community life feel inviting.
2. Verified Trust & Safety: Maintain rigorous clarity around resident verification, privacy, and spam-free safety.
3. Proximity & Discoverability: Celebrate the physical places, faces, and events within walking distance of the resident's home.
4. Champion the High Street: Give independent local merchants an elegant, organic spotlight without invasive ad banners.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast compliance across all text and interactive buttons, clear keyboard navigation, screen-reader friendly semantic structure, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
