# Hello Hyperlocal — Brand Book & Values

**Tagline:** Rediscover Where You Live.  
**Formula:** REDISCOVER → CONNECT → LOVE  
**Sub-tagline:** Rediscover your neighbourhood. Connect with the people and places in it. Love where you live.

*Hyperlocal Media & Community Platform*

---

## 1. A Simple Thought Changed Everything...

* What if we could help people rediscover their neighbourhood?
* Not through another app.
* Not through another newsletter.
* But by helping them see the place they already call home with fresh eyes.

### We Believe...
* Every neighbourhood has stories waiting to be discovered.
* Every suburb has hidden gems.
* Every street has incredible people.
* Every local business has a story.
* Most of us simply don't know they're there.

> **LET’S GO FIND THEM.**

---

## 2. Somewhere Along the Way...

We stopped discovering what was around the corner.  
* We know what's happening in New York.
* We know what's trending on TikTok.
* We know what celebrities had for breakfast.

**Yet we often don't know:**
* The café two streets away.
* The family-run bakery.
* The local artist.
* The community market.
* The neighbour changing lives.

### The Problem Isn't Community — The Problem Is Connection.
Information is everywhere, but community is fragmented across:
* Facebook
* WhatsApp
* Email
* Word of mouth

The information exists: **IT'S JUST SCATTERED.**

---

## 3. Imagine Instead...

Imagine being proud of where you live.
* Imagine discovering **one amazing local business** every week.
* Imagine **never missing** another community event.
* Imagine knowing **exactly what's happening** around you.
* Imagine supporting local because **it's easy**.

### That's Why Hello Exists:
* Not to replace communities — **To strengthen them.**
* Not to compete with local businesses — **To help them grow.**
* Not to create another social network — **TO CREATE STRONGER NEIGHBOURHOODS.**

---

## 4. Purpose, Mission & Promise

### Our Purpose
Helping people rediscover their neighbourhood. Because when people rediscover where they live... **they begin to love where they live.**

### Our Mission
To connect residents, businesses, and community organisations through one trusted local platform that celebrates everything happening around the corner.

### Our Vision
To become South Africa's leading hyperlocal neighbourhood platform—one suburb at a time.
* Connecting communities.
* Supporting local businesses.
* Celebrating neighbourhood life.

### Our Promise
**Love Where You Live.** Everything we build should make people feel more connected to where they live.

---

## 5. Our Core Beliefs

Strong neighbourhoods create stronger communities.

* **When neighbours know each other:** Communities become safer.
* **When people support local businesses:** Neighbourhoods become stronger.
* **When people discover what's around them:** Communities become more vibrant.

---

## 6. What Hello Is vs. What Hello Isn't

### What Hello IS:
A Hyperlocal neighbourhood media platform that helps people **Discover**, **Connect**, **Support**, and **Celebrate** everything local—all in one place.

### What Hello IS NOT:
* Hello isn't Facebook.
* Hello isn't a noticeboard.
* Hello isn't another directory.
* Hello isn't just an app.

> **Hello is a movement to reconnect people with the places they call home.**
