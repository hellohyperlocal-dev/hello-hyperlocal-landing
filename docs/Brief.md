# App, Landing Page & Brand Design Brief

**Tagline:** Love Where You Live.

---

## 1. Project Vision

Hello Linden is not simply a community app.  
It is a movement to reconnect people with the place they call home.  

Our mission is to inspire people to discover, support and celebrate their neighbourhood by connecting residents, local businesses and community organisations in one beautifully designed platform.  

We believe every suburb has incredible people, businesses, stories and experiences that deserve to be discovered.  

Hello Linden is the first chapter of what will become a national neighbourhood network.

### Brand Purpose
Helping people love where they live.

### Brand Mission
* To connect neighbours.
* Support local businesses.
* Celebrate community.
* Encourage discovery.
* Strengthen neighbourhood pride.

### Long-Term Vision
Build South Africa's most trusted neighbourhood platform.

**Starting with:**
* Hello Linden

**Then expanding into:**
* Hello Parkhurst
* Hello Greenside
* Hello Melville
* Hello Blairgowrie
* Hello Bryanston
* ...and eventually every suburb in South Africa.

---

## 2. Design Philosophy

### This should never feel like:
* [ ] A municipal website
* [ ] A classifieds website
* [ ] Facebook
* [ ] A community noticeboard

### Instead it should feel like:
* [x] **Apple simplicity**
* [x] **Airbnb warmth**
* [x] **Spotify friendliness**
* [x] **Instagram-quality visuals**
* [x] **Google Maps usability**
* [x] **The welcoming atmosphere of your favourite local café**

The design should make users smile when they open the app.  
Every screen should reinforce one message: **Love Where You Live.**

---

## 3. Existing Logo

The logo has already been developed.  
The designer should use it as the foundation of the visual identity.

* **The speech bubble represents:** Conversation, Community, Connection, Communication.
* **The handwritten HELLO:** Creates warmth and personality.
* **LINDEN:** Provides structure and clarity.
* **The bright green:** Creates optimism and freshness.

*The identity should evolve from this logo—not replace it.*

## 5. Emotional Experience

When someone opens Hello Linden they should feel:
> *"I didn't know that!"* > *"Let's support them."* > *"I want to go there."* > *"I'm proud to live here."* > *"I love this neighbourhood."* That feeling is more important than the technology itself.

## 6. Mobile App

The app is the heart of the platform.  
It should become part of people's daily routine:
1. Open the app.
2. Discover something nearby.
3. Support local.
4. Share with friends.
5. Repeat.

### Main Navigation
* **Home**
* **Love Local**
* **Explore**
* **What's On**
* **Share**

### Home Feed
The homepage should feel alive.  
**Examples:**
* Today's Local Specials
* Weekend Guide
* Community News
* Hidden Gems
* New Businesses
* Local Heroes
* Neighbour Stories
* Events This Week
* Love Local Picks

*The feed should prioritise beautiful imagery over text.*

### Love Local
A curated collection of:
* Restaurants
* Coffee Shops
* Retail
* Guesthouses
* Markets
* Experiences
* Featured Businesses
* Special Offers

*Everything should encourage people to spend locally.*

### Explore
Interactive map and directory.  
**Find:** Coffee, Breakfast, Hairdresser, Electrician, Doctor, Guesthouse, Pet Shop, Gym, Park—everything local.

### What's On
* Events calendar
* Markets
* School events
* Fundraisers
* Music
* Sport
* Festivals
* Community projects

### Share
Residents can submit:
* Events
* Community news
* Lost & Found
* Jobs
* Recommendations
* Business listings

*Everything is moderated before publishing.*

## 9. Illustration Style

* Simple, Playful, Modern, Neighbourhood-inspired.
* **Icons inspired by:** Coffee cups, Trees, Houses, Dogs, Maps, Shopping bags, People, Speech bubbles.

*Everything should feel friendly and approachable.*

---

## 10. Colour Palette

Use the existing logo colours as the foundation.

* **Primary:** Bright Green, Forest Green
* **Supporting:** Warm White, Soft Sage, Light Grey, Charcoal

*Large white spaces should create a fresh, uncluttered experience. Green should be reserved for positive actions and calls-to-action.*

---

## 11. Typography

* Keep the handwritten **HELLO** exclusively for branding.
* Use a modern sans-serif throughout the interface.
* Typography should be clean, highly legible and welcoming.

---

## 12. Microcopy

The language throughout the platform should be conversational.

| Standard Term | Conversational Term |
| :--- | :--- |
| Business Directory | **Explore Local** |
| Deals | **Love Local Offers** |
| Events | **What's On** |
| Community News | **Around the Neighbourhood** |
| Submit Content | **Share Something Great** |

*Every interaction should sound like it comes from a friendly neighbour.*

## 15. The Measure of Success

The success of Hello Linden will not be measured by downloads alone.

**Success is when:**
* Residents discover businesses they never knew existed.
* Local businesses grow because neighbours support them.
* Community organisations communicate more effectively.
* People become proud ambassadors for their suburb.

> **Every design decision should answer one question:** > *Does this help someone love where they live?*
