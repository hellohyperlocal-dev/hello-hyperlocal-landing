## 1. Introduction & Project Overview

Linden is already one of Johannesburg's most vibrant suburbs, boasting incredible independent businesses, beautiful parks, active community organisations, great schools, and caring residents.

### The Problem: Fragmented Communication
Important information is currently spread across multiple channels: Facebook groups, WhatsApp chats, emails, websites, and word of mouth.
* Residents often miss local events.
* Businesses struggle to reach nearby customers.
* Community organisations face challenges in communicating effectively.

### The Solution
**Hello Linden** aims to bring existing community channels together into **one trusted platform** dedicated entirely to Linden—not replacing existing channels, but unifying them.

---

## 2. Our Vision

To help people **rediscover** their neighbourhood by making it easier to discover local businesses, community events, neighbourhood news, and the people who make Linden special.

> *When people rediscover their neighbourhood, they begin to **Love Where They Live**.*

---

## 3. Why Start with Linden?

**One simple reason: It is HOME.**

* Having lived in Linden for over a decade and operated On 9th Street Guesthouse, we experience firsthand what makes this suburb unique.
* Rather than launching nationally right away, the concept should first prove itself locally, working alongside the Linden Community Association (LCA) and the wider community.

---

## 4. The Opportunity

Hello Linden will provide one trusted place where residents can:
* Discover local businesses & hidden gems
* Stay informed about community news & initiatives
* Find local events & new restaurants/cafés
* Access trusted local services & connect with community organisations
* Share opportunities with neighbours

*Everything local. All in one place.*

---

## 5. Benefits for Stakeholders

### For Residents (Free Platform)

### For Local Businesses
